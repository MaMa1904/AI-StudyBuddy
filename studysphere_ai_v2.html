Chapter 1: Atomic Structure

Chapter Summary

Atomic structure describes matter at the subatomic level. Atoms consist of protons and neutrons in a dense nucleus, surrounded by electrons in quantized energy levels called orbitals.

Electrons occupy orbitals defined by four quantum numbers (n, l, mₗ, mₛ)
Aufbau principle: electrons fill lowest energy orbitals first
Hund's rule: electrons occupy degenerate orbitals singly before pairing
Pauli exclusion: no two electrons have identical quantum numbers
